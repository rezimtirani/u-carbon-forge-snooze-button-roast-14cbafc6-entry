One line roasting an alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday: the only thing in the house with a worse work-life balance than its owner.

> My alarm clock rings at 6 AM on Saturdays — the only thing in this house with a worse work-life balance than me.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the roast to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 200 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
